Phase I — Harvest

Roll resource dice. All players collect 1 resource card per colony if the die matches an adjacent tile — unless that tile is occupied by an enemy unit.

Founders Oath holder also collects the chosen die.

Maneuver cards may be shuffled in each resource deck — if drawn, keep in hand.

Phase II — Mobilization

Move any or all of your units by their movement limit, or make attacks on enemy units.

After each attack, the opponent may perform one Counter-Action with any unused unit (move or attack), then your turn resumes.

Declare War during this phase using a War Card.

Phase III — Development

Spend resource cards to build infrastructure or recruit new units.

*New units may not act until next turn.

You may also Trade at the current exchange rate, or make Peace.

Annexing Colonies

A colony is annexed whenever an enemy land unit occupies its tile. The colony piece remains its original colour and stays annexed only while the enemy unit remains there. Once that unit leaves, the annexation ends.

While annexed, the occupying player collects any resources produced by the colony. All trade bonuses from its Roads and Port are lost — neither player gains them. Beginning one turn after annexation, the occupying player may build a Fort around the colony in their own colour; that Fort is razed if they leave.

Surrender. If all of a player's colonies are annexed, they may choose to surrender. All of that player's remaining units are removed from the board. Each annexed colony is replaced with a colony matching the colour of the player currently occupying it. All Roads and Ports belonging to the surrendering player are removed.

Exchange Rate

5:1 — trade 5 of the same resource for any 1 of your choice. Reduced by 1 for every Port (unless blockaded) OR new colony connected by road. Minimum 2:1.

*All new land/naval units must be placed in an empty, friendly, unoccupied colony or port. New units may not act until next turn.

Musketeer
2🌾 or 2🐟
Move/attack 1 tile per turn. Land unit — cannot enter water or mountain tiles.
Cavalry
2🌾+1🐟
Move/attack 2 tiles per turn. Cannot move through enemy-occupied or mountain tiles to attack. Must be placed in a colony connected by roads to another colony.
Cannon
4⚙
Move/attack 1 tile per turn. May return to original tile immediately after attacking (before opponent's counter-move). MUST return to original tile after attacking naval units. Requires a Fort at colony to purchase.
Frigate
2⚙+2🪵
Move/attack 1–2 tiles per turn. May transport one land unit across water. The land unit must start its action adjacent to the Frigate and spends that action embarking; it then rides along as the Frigate sails. On a later turn, select the passenger and click a land tile to go ashore using its own full movement range. A passenger cannot fire and cannot be targeted separately, but is lost with the ship if the Frigate is captured. Click a loaded Frigate repeatedly to cycle between ship and passenger. Requires a Port to purchase.

*Land units may not enter mountain tiles.

Colony 2🪵 2🪨 1⚙ 1🌾

Must be built 3 tiles apart, on a tile occupied by a friendly unit. May not be built on edge tiles. Allows collecting resources from adjacent tiles and buying land units.

Port 2🪵 1⚙

Must be built on a water tile adjacent to an existing colony. Allows purchasing Frigates. Decreases exchange rate by 1 (unless occupied by an enemy naval unit). Limit 1 port per colony.

Fort 3🪨 1🪵

Must be built around a colony. Protects colony: enemies must have a Cannon or Frigate PLUS 1 additional land unit adjacent for a whole turn before attacking — at which point the fort is destroyed. Allows purchasing Cannons.

Road 1🪨

Built along edges of tiles to connect colonies. Each new colony connected reduces exchange rate by 1. Units may transfer between connected colonies in 1 move/turn. Must be built upon an existing colony or road.

Bridge 1🪨 1🪵

Built on a water tile, 1 edge across — must touch land on either side. Allows land units to cross between connected bridge tiles. Also allows purchasing Cavalry.

Combat

Move any or all of your units. To attack, move your unit into your opponent's tile — their unit is injured (first hit) or captured (second hit / move into injured unit's tile).

After each resolved attack, the defending player may make one immediate Counter-Action with any unused unit (move or attack). Then the active player's turn resumes.

Forts: enemy must have a Cannon/Frigate + 1 extra land unit adjacent for a full turn before the fort is destroyed and infantry can enter.

War Cards

Each player has 3 War Cards. Declare war during Mobilization.

Gentleman's War

Attack begins next turn.

Boor's War

Attack immediately this turn. Penalty: must spend 1 extra resource of any kind per unit recruited for the rest of the game.

Casus Belli (no penalty)

If your opponent has blockaded your port, blocked your road network, or occupied tiles adjacent to your colony — you may attack immediately with no Boor's penalty.

Peace

Declare peace during Development. Recovers 1 War Card.

Maneuver cards are shuffled into the 6 resource decks. When drawn during Harvest, keep in hand. Play at the timing shown on the card. The unit must be on the tile matching the card's resource. Returned to deck after use.

🌲 Ambush (Lumber/Forest)

Play before attacking! Prevent your opponent from playing a counter card to your attack.

⚔ Outflank (Stone)

Play before moving or attacking! Take one extra move/attack with the same unit.

🛡 Retreat (Wheat)

Play once attacked! Move your attacked unit into an empty tile within its movement range.

🏰 Fortify (Mustang/Hill)

Play once attacked! Deflect enemy attack — both units remain in their original tiles.

⛰ Hideout (Iron/Mountain)

Play before moving or attacking! A unit within movement range of a Mountain tile may enter and occupy it.

⚓ Broadside (Fish/Naval)

Play before moving or attacking! Choose one of your Frigates. It may perform one additional move or attack, uninterrupted before any counter-action.

Royal Seals — Win at 8

Secret Societies (4 seals)

  • Founders Oath: First to build 2 extra colonies → choose either harvest die
  • Maritime Guild: First to build 2 ports → ports keep bonus while blockaded
  • Stonewall Society: First to build 2 forts → free fort repair
  • Crossroads Guild: First to build 2 road networks → revive with any resources

Military Badges (4 seals)

  • Musketeer Corps: Most Musketeer captures → injured Musketeers may attack
  • Cavalry Regiment: Most Cavalry captures → Cavalry moves 3 tiles
  • Artillery Order: Most Cannon captures → Cannons attack twice
  • Naval Command: Most Frigate captures → Frigates revive at port immediately

Trading Companies (6 seals)

You must hold a hex of that resource itself — a colony or port built on that terrain. Being merely adjacent is not enough, and cards alone are never enough. Then redeem 5 matching cards to found the company and claim its Royal Seal. While you hold it, any of that resource spent by other players comes to you instead of being discarded. A rival buys it out by redeeming more than you spent — you paid 5, they pay 6, and so on.

  • 🌾 Golden Grain Co. (Wheat)
  • 🐴 Northern Bounty Co. (Mustang)
  • 🐟 Colonial Roar Co. (Fish)
  • 🪨 Crown & Quill Co. (Stone)
  • 🪵 Northern Axis Co. (Lumber)
  • ⚙ Iron Mark Co. (Iron)

Domination Victory

When the board is fully colonized (no room for new colonies 3 tiles apart), a player may declare victory if they control ¾ of all colonies through building or annexing.
